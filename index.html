What are AureliaGifts wedding bags? −
They are curated wedding guest gifts designed as a beautiful alternative to the traditional wedding cake or favour. Each bag can be tailored to your celebration.
Can I customise the contents? +
Yes. Our Create Your Own option lets you discuss colours, items, personal notes and other details with our team.
How do I place an order? +
Message us on WhatsApp with your wedding date, approximate guest count and preferred collection. We'll guide you through the next steps.
Can you make bags for large weddings? +
Yes. Send us your expected quantity and event details and we'll discuss what works best for your celebration.
How far in advance should I contact you? +
The earlier the better, especially for larger quantities or custom requests. Contact us with your date and we'll advise you on timing.
